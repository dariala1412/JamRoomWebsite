3. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials (managed securely through Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email/password)

User Content

When you use the App, you may create and store:

  • Song projects including lyrics, chords, and song metadata
  • Voice recordings and audio memos
  • Project folders and organizational data

Subscription Data

  • Subscription status (Free or Pro)
  • Feature usage limits (transcription minutes, AI suggestion count)

Within the App itself, we do not collect analytics, tracking data, device identifiers, or location data. Our marketing website (jamroom.eu) uses optional analytics cookies only if you consent — see Section 15: Cookies & Website Analytics.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): To provide the App's core functionality — storing your projects, syncing data, managing your account and subscription.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For optional features such as AI-powered line suggestions and rhyme generation, which send your lyrics content to third-party AI providers for processing. You activate these features voluntarily.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the App.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Sync your content across your devices (for Pro subscribers)
  • Process voice recordings through on-device AI transcription
  • Provide AI-powered features such as line suggestions and rhyme finding
  • Manage your account and subscription
  • Send transactional emails (e.g., email confirmation on sign-up)

6. Voice Recordings & AI Processing

When you use the voice transcription feature, your audio recordings are processed using on-device AI technology (WhisperKit). Voice data is processed locally on your device and is not sent to external servers for transcription. Transcribed text is stored as part of your project data and synced to our servers only if you are a signed-in Pro subscriber.

AI-powered features (line suggestions, AI rhymes) send your lyrics text to third-party AI providers (OpenAI) for processing. This data is used solely to generate suggestions. OpenAI may retain API data for up to 30 days for abuse and safety monitoring, after which it is deleted. Your data is not used to train AI models.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Local storage: Data is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you use the App without an account, your data never leaves your device.
  • Cloud storage: Synced data (Pro feature) is stored encrypted at rest and in transit:
    • Your projects, lyrics, chords, and account information are stored on Supabase servers located in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden).
    • Voice recordings are stored on Cloudflare R2 object storage located in Eastern Europe (EU).
  • Authentication: Handled through secure third-party providers (Apple, Google) or encrypted credential storage via Supabase Auth.
  • Email service: Transactional emails are sent via Resend, which processes your email address to deliver confirmation emails.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you choose to share a project via link with collaborators.
  • Service providers: With the third-party processors listed above, solely to operate the App.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

10. User Content Confidentiality

The content you create in the App — including lyrics, voice recordings, chord data, and song projects — is private and confidential. It belongs to you.

Our team does not view, access, or use your User Content, except where you explicitly request technical support that requires us to do so. The only routine exception is our AI-powered features (line suggestions and rhyme generation): when you use them, your lyrics text is sent to OpenAI solely to generate the output you requested, and for no other purpose. Your content is never used to train AI models or for advertising.

11. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a user in the European Union, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account directly in the App under Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers.
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18): Request that we limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format. You can export your projects as text from within the App.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7): Withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing (e.g., stop using AI features).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@jamroom.eu. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde): www.dsb.gv.at

12. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • All personal data, projects, voice recordings, and associated content are permanently deleted from our live servers within 30 days.
  • Local data on your device is cleared immediately.
  • Residual copies of your data may persist in our infrastructure provider's (Supabase) automated encrypted backups for a limited period. These backups are not accessed in normal operations and are overwritten on a rolling schedule — typically within 7 to 30 days, depending on our hosting plan — after which the data is permanently gone.

13. Automated Decision-Making

The App does not engage in automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects or similarly significantly affects you (Art. 22 GDPR). AI-powered features (line suggestions, rhyme finder) provide creative suggestions only and do not make decisions on your behalf.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 14 (as set by the Austrian Datenschutzgesetz, implementing GDPR Art. 8). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 14, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.
