Questions? We're here to help

Browse the common questions below, or reach out any time — we usually reply within a day.

Do I need an account to use Jam Room?

No. Download the app and start writing right away — everything on the Free plan works with no sign-up. An account is only needed if you want Pro's cloud sync across devices.

Where are my songs stored?

On the Free plan, your Jams live entirely on your device. With Pro, they're securely backed up and synced across all your devices.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from your App Store subscription settings whenever you like, and you'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

What happens to my Pro features if I stop paying?

Your songs stay safe. You keep everything you've created and can still open and edit it — you just return to the Free plan's monthly limits on AI features and lose cloud sync.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes — the annual plan includes a 3-day free trial so you can try everything before you commit.
