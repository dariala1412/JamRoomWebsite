Delete Your Jam Room Account

App: Jam Room: Songwriting Notes · Developer: Daria Lazepko

How to delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Jam Room and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

You need an internet connection. If the deletion cannot be completed, the app tells you and nothing is removed, so you can try again.

Without the app

If you cannot open the app or no longer have it installed, email support@jamroom.eu from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my Jam Room account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete individual jams, notes and recordings at any time inside the app. Data you delete this way is removed from your device and, while you are signed in, from our servers. Your account stays active. To have specific data removed without using the app, email support@jamroom.eu and tell us what to remove.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address and sign-in details.
  • All of your jams, notes, lyrics, chords and voice recordings stored on our servers.
  • Data stored on your device is cleared when you delete the account in the app. If you delete by email, uninstalling the app removes the local copy.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your data is permanently deleted from our live servers within 30 days.
  • Residual copies may remain in our hosting provider’s (Supabase) automated encrypted backups. These are not accessed in normal operations and are overwritten on a rolling schedule, typically within 7 to 30 days, after which the data is permanently gone.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also revoke Jam Room’s access in your Google Account permissions.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy (section 12, Data Retention).
